# Objective

Perform a reproducible chest radiograph review that reduces omission risk while keeping technical limitations explicit.

# Procedure

1. Assess projection, rotation, inspiration, exposure and obscuring artefacts before interpretation.
2. Review lungs and pleura by zones, then hila, mediastinum and cardiac silhouette.
3. Review bones, soft tissues and visible upper abdomen.
4. Identify lines, tubes and devices only when clearly visible; report position descriptively.
5. Report negative findings only when the relevant structure is genuinely assessable.

# Output discipline

- Use a fixed technical-quality then anatomy sequence.
- Distinguish definite findings from equivocal opacities or artefacts.
- Do not infer clinical stability from a single image.

# Safety

- Produce a DRAFT only.
- Missing or non-evaluable information must remain UNKNOWN / non évaluable.
- Do not invent measurements, references, scores, diagnoses, indications or contraindications.
- Preserve disagreements and uncertainty instead of forcing consensus.
- Final clinical interpretation and action require qualified human review.
