# Objective

Apply a repeatable abdominal CT search pattern and preserve protocol-dependent limitations.

# Procedure

1. Record contrast phase information if supplied and mark it UNKNOWN otherwise.
2. Review liver, biliary tree, pancreas, spleen, adrenals and kidneys.
3. Review bowel, mesentery, peritoneum and retroperitoneum.
4. Review major vessels, lymph nodes, abdominal wall and visible osseous structures.
5. Separate incidental findings from the primary imaging question without suppressing potentially important observations.

# Output discipline

- State phase-dependent limitations explicitly.
- Do not infer enhancement kinetics when phases are unavailable.
- Keep urgency and treatment recommendations for human review.

# Safety

- Produce a DRAFT only.
- Missing or non-evaluable information must remain UNKNOWN / non évaluable.
- Do not invent measurements, references, scores, diagnoses, indications or contraindications.
- Preserve disagreements and uncertainty instead of forcing consensus.
- Final clinical interpretation and action require qualified human review.
