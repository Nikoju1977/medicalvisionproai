# Objective

Structure an acute neuroimaging safety screen without declaring time-sensitive treatment eligibility.

# Procedure

1. Verify modality, coverage and major artefacts.
2. Look for intracranial haemorrhage, mass effect, midline shift and hydrocephalus.
3. Assess for visible territorial or focal parenchymal abnormality appropriate to the supplied sequences.
4. Review extra-axial spaces, ventricles and visible skull or sinuses when evaluable.
5. Escalate any uncertainty that could materially alter urgent human interpretation.

# Output discipline

- State which acute hazards were or were not evaluable.
- Keep treatment windows and intervention criteria outside the skill unless explicit dated evidence is supplied.
- Require immediate human review for potentially critical findings.

# Safety

- Produce a DRAFT only.
- Missing or non-evaluable information must remain UNKNOWN / non évaluable.
- Do not invent measurements, references, scores, diagnoses, indications or contraindications.
- Preserve disagreements and uncertainty instead of forcing consensus.
- Final clinical interpretation and action require qualified human review.
