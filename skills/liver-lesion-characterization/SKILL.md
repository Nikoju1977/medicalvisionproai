# Objective

Characterise a focal liver lesion without assuming a contrast phase or assigning a definitive pathology from incomplete imaging.

# Procedure

1. Confirm which contrast phases or MRI sequences are present.
2. Describe size from calibrated data, segment/location when supportable, margins and baseline signal or density.
3. Describe enhancement pattern only across phases actually supplied.
4. Look for associated biliary, vascular, capsular or extrahepatic features.
5. Provide a constrained differential and identify the missing phase, sequence, laboratory or prior imaging information that matters.

# Output discipline

- Never invent wash-in, washout or capsule when the relevant phase is absent.
- Keep formal staging and treatment implications outside the skill.
- Preserve indeterminate status when imaging criteria are incomplete.

# Safety

- Produce a DRAFT only.
- Missing or non-evaluable information must remain UNKNOWN / non évaluable.
- Do not invent measurements, references, scores, diagnoses, indications or contraindications.
- Preserve disagreements and uncertainty instead of forcing consensus.
- Final clinical interpretation and action require qualified human review.
