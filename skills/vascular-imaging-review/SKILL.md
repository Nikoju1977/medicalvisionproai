# Objective

Structure vascular imaging observations without turning image estimates into treatment thresholds.

# Procedure

1. Confirm vascular territory, modality and contrast or Doppler technique.
2. Assess technical adequacy and artefacts that may mimic stenosis or occlusion.
3. Describe patency, calibre change, filling defect, dissection flap or aneurysmal morphology only when visible.
4. Use quantitative stenosis or diameter values only when the measurement method is available and calibrated.
5. Review relevant branch vessels and downstream effects within the covered field.

# Output discipline

- State measurement method or mark it UNKNOWN.
- Do not apply intervention thresholds without dated evidence and clinician context.
- Escalate potentially critical vascular findings for immediate human review.

# Safety

- Produce a DRAFT only.
- Missing or non-evaluable information must remain UNKNOWN / non évaluable.
- Do not invent measurements, references, scores, diagnoses, indications or contraindications.
- Preserve disagreements and uncertainty instead of forcing consensus.
- Final clinical interpretation and action require qualified human review.
