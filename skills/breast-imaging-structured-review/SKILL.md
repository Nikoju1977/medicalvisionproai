# Objective

Describe breast imaging findings consistently while keeping formal category assignment dependent on complete protocol and specialist validation.

# Procedure

1. Identify modality, views or sequences and whether the examination is complete for the intended question.
2. Describe masses, asymmetries, calcifications, architectural distortion or enhancement using observable features.
3. Document location and measurements only from calibrated or supplied data.
4. Compare with prior imaging when available and record change explicitly.
5. List missing views, sequences or correlation needed before categorical assessment.

# Output discipline

- Use descriptive imaging language before any category terminology.
- Do not fabricate a BI-RADS category or recommendation from incomplete inputs.
- Require breast-imaging specialist review.

# Safety

- Produce a DRAFT only.
- Missing or non-evaluable information must remain UNKNOWN / non évaluable.
- Do not invent measurements, references, scores, diagnoses, indications or contraindications.
- Preserve disagreements and uncertainty instead of forcing consensus.
- Final clinical interpretation and action require qualified human review.
